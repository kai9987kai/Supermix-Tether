03 / RESEARCH BOUNDARY

Measure useful dependence.

Tether combines relevant intervention-effect matching with invariance to irrelevant and damaged-expert changes. The primary comparison is identical-data interchange-intervention training (IIT).

Real images, small domain

1,797 sklearn digits. Image identities split before task generation. This is a within-dataset split.

Controlled challenges

Withheld source × operation pairs, proxy-cue shifts, donor erasure and synthetic expert corruption.

This is a bounded experimental model. Accuracy, routing weights and checksums do not establish semantic understanding, general intelligence or real-world safety.
