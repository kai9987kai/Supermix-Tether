05 / RESEARCH BOUNDARY

Measure useful dependence.

Tether combines relevant intervention-effect matching with invariance to irrelevant and damaged-expert changes, augmented in v0.4 with Split Conformal Prediction (RAPS), Axiomatic Integrated Gradients, and Causal Interchange Patching.

Real images, small domain

1,797 sklearn digits. Image identities split before task generation. Conformal calibration uses the validation split only.

Controlled challenges

Withheld source × operation pairs, proxy-cue shifts, donor erasure and 1/3, 2/3, and 3/3 specialist corruption.

This is a bounded experimental model. Accuracy, routing weights, conformal sets and checksums do not establish semantic understanding, general intelligence or real-world safety.
